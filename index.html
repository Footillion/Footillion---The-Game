    HOW TO PLAY

    Every day there is a new round of 7 football prompts. Type an answer that fits each prompt before the 25 second clock runs out.

    You get one attempt per day. The rarer your correct answer, the more points you score. Everyone gets the same questions on the same day.

    Sunday League10Everyone says it
    Offside15Obvious, but a step beyond
    Squad Player30Pretty common
    First Teamer60Knowledgeable fans
    World Class85Deep cut
    One in a Footillion100Super rare!

    Each answer runs you down the pitch: 10 stays at the top, 15 is offside, 30 is level with the fans, 60 with the dugout, 85 is through on goal and 100 is a goal. An answer that does not fit the prompt scores 0. A perfect round is 700.

    TIP: Skip the obvious. Typing Manchester United for a Premier League club is safe, but Oldham Athletic is gold. Small typos are forgiven.

    MULTIPLAYER: open the menu to play 1-8 players on one device and see who scores highest.
